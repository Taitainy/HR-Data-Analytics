# HR-Data-Analytics
This project cotains interactive dashboards which provide a holistic HR analytics view that  shows the workforce structure, reveals employee sentiment and performance, and highlights development efforts.
## Key Insights
### Workforce Demography Overview
This analysis provides a clear snapshot of the organization’s employee composition. With a total of 2,845 employees, the average age is relatively high at 49.4 years, and the average tenure is 5.28 years, suggesting a mature and experienced workforce. Gender distribution is fairly balanced, with 56% male and 44% female employees. The age group analysis shows that adults form the largest segment, while younger employees are fewer, which may indicate challenges in attracting younger talent. Departmental distribution reveals that Production dominates with 1,910 employees, followed by IT/IS and Sales, while the Executive Office has the smallest headcount. Business units are evenly staffed, each hovering around 280–290 employees, reflecting a balanced organizational structure across divisions.
### Employee Performance & Engagement Dashboard
This shifts focus to employee sentiment and performance. Engagement, satisfaction, work-life balance, and employee ratings all hover around the mid-range (between 2.9 and 3.0), signaling moderate levels of employee morale. The engagement distribution shows that just over half of employees fall into the “moderate” category, with only about a quarter highly engaged, which could be a concern for long-term productivity. Departmental performance scores highlight Admin Offices as the top-performing group, while the Executive Office lags behind. Satisfaction scores vary widely across divisions, with Engineers and General Contractors showing strong numbers, while smaller divisions like Splicing and Wireline trail behind. Overall, the dashboard suggests that while performance is steady, there is room for improvement in boosting engagement and satisfaction across the board.
